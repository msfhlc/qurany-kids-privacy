🗑️ Delete Your Account

Request deletion of your Qurany Kids account and all associated data

What data will be deleted?

When you request account deletion, the following data will be permanently removed:

  • Account Information: Email address, display name, profile picture
  • Child Profiles: All child profiles you created (names, ages, avatars)
  • Learning Progress: Quran memorization progress, achievements, points
  • App Data: Settings, preferences, favorites, recordings
  • Aquarium Data: Fish collection, aquarium progress

⚠️ Warning

Account deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. All your children's learning progress and achievements will be lost forever.

How to Delete Your Account

1
Send us an email

Use the email address associated with your Qurany Kids account

2
Include your account email

Mention the email you used to sign in to the app

3
Request deletion

State that you want to delete your account and all associated data

Send your deletion request to:

msuf@halfbloodstudio.com

Email Template

You can copy and use this template:

Subject: Account Deletion Request - Qurany Kids Hello, I would like to request the deletion of my Qurany Kids account and all associated data. Account Email: [YOUR EMAIL HERE] Please confirm once my account has been deleted. Thank you.

Processing Time: Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days. You will receive a confirmation email once your data has been deleted.

Alternative: Clear Local Data

If you just want to clear data from your device without deleting your cloud account:

  • Uninstall the Qurany Kids app from your device
  • This will remove all locally stored data
  • Your cloud data will remain if you want to use the app again later

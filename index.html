🕌 Qurany Kids - Privacy Policy

Last Updated: December 27, 2024

Introduction

Qurany Kids ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application designed to help children learn and memorize the Quran.

Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address (when signing in with Google or Email)
  • Display name
  • Profile picture (from Google account, if applicable)

Child Profile Information

  • Child's name (as entered by parent)
  • Age
  • Selected avatar
  • Learning progress and achievements

Usage Data

  • Quran memorization progress
  • Study time and activity
  • App preferences and settings

Microphone Permission

Important: Our app requests microphone access to enable the recitation verification feature. This allows children to practice Quran recitation and receive feedback.

Audio is processed locally on your device using browser speech recognition technology and is NOT recorded, stored, or transmitted to any server.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and maintain our service
  • To sync your progress across devices
  • To track learning progress and achievements
  • To improve our app and user experience
  • To send important notifications about app updates

Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your information. Data is stored on your device locally and synced to the cloud when you sign in with your account.

Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication - For user sign-in and authentication
  • Firebase Firestore - For cloud data storage and sync
  • Firebase Analytics - For app usage analytics (anonymized)
  • Quran.com API - For Quran text and audio content
  • AlQuran.cloud API - For Quran translations

Children's Privacy

Qurany Kids is designed for use by children under parental supervision. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children.

All account creation and management should be done by a parent or guardian. Child profiles are created and managed by the parent account holder.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account and all associated data at any time by contacting us. Local data can be cleared by uninstalling the app.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.
